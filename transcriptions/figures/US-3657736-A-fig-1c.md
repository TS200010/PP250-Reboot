## Patent prose — PRIMARY EVIDENCE, separately attributed

The introductory device description identifies RU, CU, store CS, AU and HSE as parts of one device served by internal parallel highways through HIC. Empty circles are micro-signal-controlled gates; detailed gate-control wires are omitted and grouped at CU's CONTROL SIGNALS output.

The arithmetic-unit description says AU provides conventional ADD, SUBTRACT, SHIFT and similar operations. It loads/unloads through the internal highway and supplies condition signals to CU over AUCS. These operations are not separately expanded inside AU.

The control-unit description says FUR and internal conditions control CU's generation of sequenced control signals. The instruction-format discussion identifies a seven-bit function field. That gives context for the 1/7 FUR endpoints; the other instruction fields are not drawn in FUR here.

The highway-station description says:

- HSE consists of a common buffer unit BU and one control-logic unit for each highway pair.
- In the described highway system, each highway has fifteen lines: ten data, four code and one strobe.
- A highway is a ring passing through a control circuit at each connected device; stations normally pass/relaunch signals except during sending or receiving.
- BDR supplies data characters and BCR supplies codes for transfers.
- HSCCC decodes highway codes and supplies conditions to CU.
- Highway-station-ready and highway-station-accept indications are not separately shown, but are included in HSECS.

Those statements explain the equipment's role and signal widths. They do not turn omitted ring closures, detailed register connections or individual ready/accept wires into visible Figure 1c connections.

The prose also says other highway systems could be used. The particular fifteen-line organisation is the described embodiment, not a drawing-based requirement for every implementation.

## Uncertainties and source limits

- Figure 1a appears to label the CPB zero output BPA=0, while this figure clearly prints CPB=0. The discrepancy is retained across the separate captures.
- No individual names are printed for the two illustrated HSCCC condition strokes or the HSE control arrows. Ready/accept membership is supplied by prose only.
- HSR has no drawn connections; its functions are not invented from its name.
- The BU-to-highway-controller wiring is not expanded, so a complete transmitter/receiver datapath cannot be traced inside HSE from this figure alone.
- Endpoint indices abbreviate parallel paths. Ten-bit internal and fifteen-line external organisation are supported by prose, not counts of every drawn wire.
- The grouped CONTROL SIGNALS and AU CONTROL SIGNALS do not disclose control timing, instruction microcode or gate-enable arbitration.
- File separation follows the user's request while preserving the source's shared-device context.
