## Evidence and notation

**PRIMARY EVIDENCE / DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION:** Sections headed “Drawing” describe visible marks, labels and paths. “Prose clarification” explicitly supplies meanings or operations from the patent text. No architectural reconstruction or modern implementation is added.

Circles contain either **2** or **1**. The distinct reference labels beside selected circles are **G1–G19** and **GS**. A circle's internal 2 is not a reference numeral “G2”. Several circles have no separate G-reference. Small oblique arrows enter the circles marked 2. The prose, paragraph (14), identifies 2 as AND gating under micro-program control and 1 as OR isolation; it also explains that the single drawn leads represent parallel paths, typically 24 bits. That width is supplied by prose, not a bus-width annotation on these two sheets.

Braces with two or more arrows represent grouped connections, not necessarily that number of individual data bits. Crossing lines are not automatically junctions. The drawing is a block/data-path diagram, not a complete pin-level netlist.

## Drawing: layout and components

The upper control rectangle spans the sheet join. Its split label reads **μPROG** when the halves are read together. Above its left portion are grouped inward arrows **AUCS**, **ICS**, **PCS**, **CIS**. On the right is **IR**, connected to the control block by a grouped path from IR's left side into the top of μPROG. An unnumbered 2-gate to the right of IR takes an upward branch from **SOH** and feeds IR.

Across the middle of the combined drawing, from left to right, are:

- **ACC STK**, with side selector **SELA**;
- **BASE STK**, with side selector **SELB**;
- **SSCR**, with a small **SF** block immediately above and connected into its input grouping;
- **RES REG**;
- **OP REG** (called OPREG in prose);
- **TC / LMT STK**, with side selector **SELL**;
- **PGC**, farther right.

Lower down, **MILL** is on the left; **MIP** and **MIS** lie to its right but still in Fig. 2a. **COMP** is in the lower left of Fig. 2b; **HIS STK**, with selector **SELH**, is to its right; **SDIREG** is below it. Rectangles for registers/stacks show grouped input and output arrows. TC and LMT share a stack outline with an internal dividing line; SELL is a narrow attached selection section.

Prose clarification, paragraph (12): these are respectively the accumulator/working register stack, capability base stack, special block capability register, result register, operand register, capability type-code/limit stack, parity generation/comparison circuit, arithmetic unit, primary/secondary machine indicator registers, comparator, historic register stack, and memory data input register. These expanded names are not all printed inside the drawing.

## Drawing: highways and principal feedback loop

**MHW** is the heavy main path. MILL's bottom output joins a heavy line running right along the bottom of Fig. 2a, continuing into the bottom-left of Fig. 2b, turning upward beside the operand/limit area, then left across the upper register row and back into Fig. 2a. Its upper portion feeds G2, G19, G13 and G17; its right vertical portion feeds G4. Thus the bottom and upper MHW stretches are portions of one return path, not two separate highways. The visible arrows at the branches establish flow into the gated destinations.

At the far right, **SOH** enters from outside the drawing with grouped leftward arrows. A branch feeds IR through an unnumbered 2-gate. Another passes left through **GS**, then runs left across the diagram to the register-loading paths. SOH also has a branch down into PGC. Its label is distinct from **SOHCS**, the adjacent grouped memory-control connection entering μPROG and supplying the parity-related branches.

**SIH** leaves rightward from SDIREG through G5. **SIHCS** leaves rightward separately from μPROG, with its line descending along the right of the drawing before its outward arrow. SDIREG output also branches back left toward COMP and upward into the PGC checking arrangement.

- **AUμS** enters MILL from the left as a grouped pair; **AUCS** leaves MILL at the right and is also shown entering μPROG at the top.

- **ICS** comprises leftward grouped outputs from MIP and MIS, and reappears at μPROG's top input. MIP also has an output marked **F** routed into the MIS input area.
- **SIμCS** enters MIS from above.

- **BPB** runs from the narrow parity-associated side of BASE STK to COMP. **LPB** is printed beside the corresponding path below TC/LMT STK, also entering COMP. COMP additionally receives the gated base and limit data and a long return from SDIREG on its right.
- **SOHCS** supplies a downward branch into **IP**. IP has a leftward-side input labelled **PS** (arrow directed into IP); its downward output, labelled **SPB**, branches left toward G8, down through G10, and into PGC.

Prose clarification: paragraph (33) identifies μPGCS as timed gate-control signals, AUμS as MILL operation controls, CμS as comparator controls, FIS as primary fault-bit controls, SIμCS as secondary-indicator controls, INC as historic-selector stepping, and +1S as increment control. Paragraphs (37)–(41) describe COMP's base/limit/access checks, SDIREG's outgoing address/data role, and PGC's comparisons. Paragraph (70) explains PS as parity-state control from MIS, controlling IP and informing COMP and PGC. These meanings and timing are not fully specified by the arrows alone.

## Prose clarification: examples that identify the drawn routes

These are patent statements, not additional wires inferred into the diagram:

- Paragraphs (97) and (102): GS/G7/G8 load the selected base half and its parity; GS/G9/G10 load the selected limit half and its parity.
- Paragraphs (104)–(105): G11/G12 feed base and limit values to MILL for a local sum-check, with G13 storing the result; G14/G15 support comparison with the word in OPREG through MILL.
- Paragraph (107): G16/G17/G18 transfer saved indicators from ACC STK through MILL/MHW and OPREG into HIS STK.
- Paragraph (120): G11/G4/G5 support an outgoing dump-area access; G13 saves the address, and G14/G13 participate in updating it through MILL.
- Paragraph (131): G3/G19 and MILL increment the store-module field of SSCR.
- Paragraph (90): SF is a hard-wired strapping field setting the fixed fault-block address portion of SSCR. The small block itself is labelled only SF in the drawing.
- Paragraphs (18), (20), (25): HIS STK's queue dimensions and record contents, the named capability-register entries, and the allocation of type and limit fields are prose/other-figure details. They are not expanded into fictitious internal registers on this Figure 2 capture.

## Uncertainties and source discrepancies

1. **Unnumbered gates:** the four 2-gates listed above genuinely lack readable separate G-reference labels. Their locations and connections are recorded without invented identifiers.
2. **Grouped controls:** μPGCS, AUCS, ICS, PCS, CIS and the bracketed input/output groups are not pin-level expansions. The exact individual control wires, gate enable timing and all indicator-bit connections cannot be recovered from these two sheets alone.
3. **Dense parity/comparator area:** BPB and LPB can be traced to COMP, but the scan does not justify a bit-by-bit allocation of the adjacent data/parity inputs. At closely intersecting lines near PGC, COMP and the sheet join, only the continuous paths and explicit branches described above are asserted.
4. **G6/G17 prose conflict:** the drawing places G17 on the MHW-to-OP REG path and G6 on the SOH/GS-to-OP REG path. Paragraph (92) uses GS/G6 for a received word, agreeing with that drawing; paragraph (116) instead says GS/G17 for a received word. This capture preserves the drawing's assignments and records the prose disagreement rather than silently exchanging the gate labels.
5. **Other transcription discrepancies:** paragraph (90) lists G3/G4/G6 in an address-forming operation, while its following description and the drawn SDIREG-to-SIH path identify G5 as the output gate. Paragraph (116) also calls the checking block PCG; the drawing reads PGC. These observations do not establish whether the discrepancies arose in the original patent or its transcription.
6. **Reading conventions:** Greek μ and spacing have been normalized for search (μPROG, μPGCS, AUμS, CμS, SIμCS; OP REG/OPREG and TC/LMT STK). No additional control function is inferred from typography. The split μPGCS brace should not be interpreted as one individually traceable enable wire.
