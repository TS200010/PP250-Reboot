## Direct drawing evidence — PRIMARY EVIDENCE / DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION

### Layout and grouping

A tall input-grouping brace stands on the left, labelled on three lines:

`OP / REG / O/P`

It spans twenty-four individually drawn input lines, with endpoint indices 0 at the top and 23 at the bottom. Four circular gates, GZD1, GZD2, GZD3 and GZD4, are stacked vertically to the right of those inputs. Each collects six input strokes.

Their four outputs feed a fifth circular gate, GZD5, farther right. GZD5 feeds a small square symbol labelled IZD. A final rightward arrow is labelled OP=0, with (µCS) below it.

There is no enclosing ZD rectangle, operand-register storage symbol or microprogram-control-unit block. OP REG O/P labels the source grouping; it does not draw the operand register itself.

### Input grouping and arrow directions

The twenty-four left-hand strokes are organised into four consecutive groups of six, top to bottom:

| Drawn input group | Destination | Printed indexing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First six strokes | GZD1 | Top endpoint is marked 0. |
| Second six strokes | GZD2 | No individual bit indices printed. |
| Third six strokes | GZD3 | No individual bit indices printed. |
| Last six strokes | GZD4 | Bottom endpoint is marked 23. |

Each group has the same general fan-in geometry:

1. The uppermost line runs right, then turns downward into the gate from above.
2. The next line runs right and slopes down toward an upper-left input.
3. The next line approaches a left/upper-left input.
4. The next approaches a left/lower-left input.
5. The next slopes upward into a lower-left input.
6. The lowest line runs right and turns upward into the gate from below.

Arrowheads point into each gate. These are six distinct incoming lines, not six branches of one shared conductor. The long left brace is grouping, not a common electrical connection.

Intermediate bit numbers 1–22 are not printed. Assigning the consecutive groups to bits 0–5, 6–11, 12–17 and 18–23 is an ordered interpretation of the endpoint indices and six-line grouping, not a set of explicit per-wire labels.

### All first-stage outputs and convergence at GZD5

- GZD1’s output leaves its right side, runs right and then downward, ending in a downward arrowhead at GZD5’s top input.
- GZD2’s output leaves right, bends downward and then diagonally right/down, ending in an arrowhead at GZD5’s upper-left input.
- GZD3’s output leaves right, bends upward and then diagonally right/up, ending in an arrowhead at GZD5’s lower-left input.
- GZD4’s output leaves right and turns upward along the long lower route, ending in an upward arrowhead at GZD5’s bottom input.

The four routes remain distinct until the four-input GZD5 symbol. No first-stage gate feeds another first-stage gate. There is no output feedback to the operand-input lines.

### Final inversion and external output

A horizontal line connects GZD5’s right side to IZD’s left side. IZD’s right-side output continues horizontally to a rightward arrow labelled OP=0 (µCS).

There is no branch between GZD5 and IZD, no alternate final output, and no drawn connection onward to a µPROG block. The visible order is:

`four six-input stages → GZD5 → IZD → OP=0 (µCS)`

## Patent prose — PRIMARY EVIDENCE, separately attributed

Paragraph (54) explicitly identifies:

- GZD1–GZD4 as four six-input NOR gates.
- GZD5 as a four-input NAND gate.
- IZD as an inverter.
- The input as the twenty-four-bit output of OPREG, partitioned into four groups of six.
- The final significant output as the zero-condition signal sent to the microprogram control unit.

The prose explains that each NOR output becomes one when all six inputs in its group are zero. If all twenty-four operand bits are zero, all four NOR outputs are one, GZD5’s NAND output is zero, and IZD produces one on OP=0.

The Step SZ account, paragraph (113), explicitly describes the other case: if any operand input is one, its NOR output becomes zero, the NAND output becomes one, and the final OP=0 output becomes zero.

Thus the prose-supported functional table is:

| OPREG contents | Four NOR outputs | GZD5 NAND output | Final OP=0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All twenty-four bits zero | All one | Zero | One |
| At least one bit one | At least one zero | One | Zero |

These logic names and state descriptions come from the prose. They are not additional worded legends printed beside the gate symbols.

Paragraphs (112)–(114) place the detector in a load-capability microsequence: µPROG tests the signal to determine whether the sum-check word is zero. That use does not make Figure 11 itself a complete sum-check computation circuit. The input drawn here remains OPREG’s output.

## Uncertainties and source limits

- **Scan degradation:** A pale band near GZD4 interrupts portions of the left brace, some incoming strokes and its long output route. Their alignment, remaining segments and gate-entry arrowheads make the intended six-input/fourth-output route discernible, but the damaged ink is not described as perfectly continuous.
- **Bit indexing:** Only 0 and 23 are printed. Intermediate per-wire numbering is not supplied as direct drawing evidence.
- **GZD5 symbol:** The overbarred 4 is preserved literally; its identification as NAND and its four-input role are explicit in the prose.
- **Output glyph:** OP=0/OP=O is a zero-test label in context; no distinct letter-O condition is inferred.
- **IZD:** The visible square/V symbol is retained, with “inverter” supplied by the prose rather than a modern replacement symbol inserted into the capture.
- **Timing and implementation:** No gate delays, hazards, synchronisation, clocking or electrical levels are specified here. No latch is inferred at the output.
- **Architectural scope:** The figure shows the detector network, not operand-register loading, the arithmetic producing a sum-check, or subsequent microprogram branching. These limits do not declare those mechanisms unresolved elsewhere in the repository.

INTERPRETIVE NOTE

**Explicit figure evidence / DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION:** Four six-input groups converge through separately routed gate outputs into GZD5, then IZD, yielding one labelled condition output.

**Explicit patent-text evidence / DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION:** The stages implement four NORs, a NAND and an inverter, with a final one precisely when all twenty-four OPREG bits are zero.

**Architectural inference:** This network supplies a word-wide zero test to control sequencing. The depicted use of OPREG makes it a test of the value currently presented there, not independently drawn hardware that validates the capability descriptor or computes its sum-check.
