# Plessey / Ptarmigan / PP250 — archival research leads

**Recorded:** 18 October 2026  
**Status:** Research leads only; archive custody, holdings and access require confirmation.  
**Purpose:** Locate original Plessey System 250 / PP250 engineering documentation, especially processor logic diagrams, wiring, datapaths, sequencing/microprogramming documentation, maintenance manuals and software.

## Priority investigation: Ptarmigan and RAE Farnborough

Investigate the suggestion that the UK defence **Ptarmigan** programme used PP250 equipment and that **RAE Farnborough may have had a machine**. The specific Farnborough installation is **not confirmed** and must not be treated as an established fact.

- Search Ptarmigan programme and procurement files, technical reports, acceptance tests, system configuration and equipment inventories.
- Search RAE Farnborough computing/equipment registers, installation and maintenance records and test reports.
- Determine whether records remain with the Ministry of Defence, were transferred to The National Archives or were destroyed.
- Enquire about original supplier-delivered Plessey engineering documents and drawings, not just programme summaries.

**Initial routes:** [The National Archives Discovery](https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/) and [MoD enquiries / FOI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/contact-the-ministry-of-defence). Search alternative names including *Ptarmigan*, *Plessey*, *System 250*, *PP250* and *RAE Farnborough*.

**Retention caution:** UK public-record transfer/review arrangements do not guarantee preservation of an individual technical file. Absence from Discovery does not establish destruction: records may be retained, uncatalogued in a useful way, or filed within broader programme series. Request a search for archive custody and surviving file series before requesting particular documents.

## Proposed sequence

1. Check Discovery for Ptarmigan/RAE records and identify record-series references and closure/retention status.
2. Ask MoD records staff which archive holds Ptarmigan technical and RAE Farnborough equipment files.
3. Contact IET and Science Museum Group to establish the scope of their Plessey collections and request referrals.
4. Follow the Liverpool, Rochester, Lewisham and corporate-successor leads.
5. Capture catalogue identifiers, exact titles, dates, access conditions and provenance before drawing conclusions.

## Evidence sought

Highest-value finds would be original **PP250 circuit and logic drawings**, processor control matrix/microcode details, signal and gate diagrams, cabinet/module interconnection schematics, engineering change documentation and hardware maintenance manuals. Useful secondary evidence includes installation inventories, project system descriptions, operating-system sources and contemporary photographs.

**Unresolved archival question:** Where, if anywhere, is the original Plessey computer engineering archive held? No central custodian has yet been established.

This is a *source-recovery task*, not an additional architectural unknown in `research/pp250-open-questions.md`.
