# PP250 people — public contact routes

## Purpose

This is a deliberately small working list of **people for whom the project has actually identified a usable public contact route** in connection with PP250/System 250 historical and archival research.

It is not a list of everyone we would like to contact. A person belongs here only when there is a concrete route such as a confirmed public professional profile, published professional email, institutional affiliation, publisher/author route, or an organisation that can plausibly forward correspondence.

The intended use is respectful historical outreach: Anthony John Stanners, a former member of the System 250/CORAL 250 team, is assembling an archive of the machine, its documentation and the people who worked on it, and would like to invite recollections, surviving documents and leads to other former team members.

Because this repository is public, do **not** record private residential addresses, private telephone numbers, or personal details obtained only from people-search/data-broker services. A professional correspondence address may be recorded when the person or organisation has published it for that purpose.

## Admission rule

Add an entry only after both of these are established:

1. the person is identified with sufficient confidence; and
2. there is an actual public route through which correspondence can reasonably be attempted.

Do not add placeholder rows for people whose contact information has not been found.

Where identity is uncertain, resolve the identity before adding the contact route. Where a route is indirect, say so explicitly.
